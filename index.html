About

I am a forensic microbiologist and computational biologist working on pathogen detection, portable genomics, and bioinformatics.

I hold a Ph.D. in Plant Protection from São Paulo State University (UNESP) and have over a decade of experience in molecular biology, next-generation sequencing, and bioinformatics.

I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Entomology and Plant Pathology at Oklahoma State University (OSU) and a Senior Scientist at MiFi, Inc., where I develop computational tools for metagenomic pathogen detection.
